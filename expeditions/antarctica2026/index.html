Lake Untersee Oasis Expedition 2026 —

An otherworldly analog at the ends of the Earth

EXPEDITION DATES

October - December 2026

LOCATION

Lake Untersee, Queen Maud Land, Antarctica

SCIENCE GOALS

Investigating how microbial life adapts to the extreme conditions beneath the Antarctic ice, and studying Lake Untersee as an analogue for icy ocean worlds such as Enceladus.

TECHNOLOGY GOALS

Developing and evaluating low-cost robotic technologies for under-ice exploration, including underwater navigation, imaging, sonar-based sensing, photogrammetry, and 3D mapping.

Expedition overview

Lake Untersee is a remote Antarctic lake located in Queen Maud Land, surrounded by the mountains and glaciers of the Gruber Mountains. This unique polar environment is one of the closest analogues to Enceladus that we have here in Earth. The expedition, in the November / December 2026 polar season, is focused on answering major astrobiology and ecology questions about the Lake Untersee environment.

The expedition brings together scientific exploration and robotic fieldwork to investigate this extreme environment. Our work focuses on using robotic systems and imaging technologies to collect spatial observations and develop detailed maps of the lake environment.

Scientific objectives

The expedition aims to support scientific investigations of Lake Untersee through robotic exploration and mapping.

Key objectives include:

  • Characterising the underwater environment through imaging and spatial mapping.
  • Collecting observations to support scientific investigations of the lake.
  • Investigating how robotic systems can support scientific exploration in remote polar environments.
  • Developing datasets and mapping products that can support future research.
